Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026 · Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Logged ("the App") is a personal record-keeping application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

The App is designed with privacy at its core. All data you enter — including text notes, photos, and location data — is stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or share your personal records with any server, third party, or cloud service.

  • Text entries: Stored locally in your browser's localStorage
  • Photos: Resized and stored as base64 in localStorage on your device only
  • Location: GPS coordinates captured only when you enable this feature; stored locally only

3. Permissions Requested

  • Camera / Photo Library: Required only when you tap "Attach Photo." Images are stored locally only.
  • Microphone: Required only when you enable "Voice Memo." Audio is recorded and stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted.
  • Location: Required only when you enable GPS logging. Used solely to capture coordinates at the time of entry.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data. Your records never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export them (e.g., PDF export to your own files).

5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time from within the app. You can delete all data via Settings → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the app will also remove all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The updated date will be reflected at the top. Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@loggedapp.io
